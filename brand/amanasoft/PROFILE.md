## What the samples taught us

1. **Sample 1 (square, wave layout)** is the strongest: clean, on-brand and easy to read. It is the model for the default "Wave" template.
2. **Sample 2 (tall poster)** has too much text for a social feed, and its footer says **amanasoft.net**, but the real site is **amanasoft.ae**.
3. **Sample 4 (dark AI theme)** has a typo ("reducerd") and a **wrong phone number** (+971 59408284, missing digits). These are the typical mistakes of AI-written text inside images.

➡ This is why our system has the AI make only the **picture**. The headline, logo and contact details are drawn by code, so they are always spelled correctly and always match this profile.
